About

I am a high school student and developer. I am currently working through Harvard’s CS50 Introduction to Computer Science on edX, building web projects in TypeScript and JavaScript, and running my own home server — Home Assistant, a Plex media server, and my own Pi-hole server — because I like knowing how things work and making them work myself. My curiosity does not stick to one subject: it jumps from physics and chemistry to health science, media literacy, criminal law, design, Photoshop, and cooking.
